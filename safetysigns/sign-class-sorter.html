Sign classes follow AS 1319 Safety signs for the occupational environment. Symbols are from the MSL944002 teaching slides.

Teacher notes for this pilot

This panel is for the pilot only. Students will not see it in the released version.

What this is

A prototype of the first activity in a planned library of small, reusable OH&S activities. It takes about five minutes. Round 1 asks students to sort 12 signs into their class by shape and colour. Round 2 asks what 8 of those signs mean. A new set of signs is drawn each time it is played, so it can be repeated.

Where it would sit

Week 2, Introduction to OHS, on the after-class page, just before the practice quiz. It could also be embedded in any other course that covers safety signs, since the content set can be swapped for workshop or field signs.

Five things to check

  1. Classes. The slides teach four classes and group the fire extinguisher and fire blanket under emergency information. AS 1319 treats fire signs as their own class, so this version uses five. Keep five, or go back to four?
  2. Wording. Are the meanings in Round 2 worded the way you teach them? Anything you would change?
  3. Length. Is 12 signs then 8 questions the right length, or should it be shorter?
  4. Hints. A wrong drop shows the shape and colour only. After a second wrong drop it gives the answer. Is that the right amount of help?
  5. Gaps. Which signs do students actually confuse in the lab that are missing here?

What a successful trial looks like

  • The teacher signs off the sign meanings and class names.
  • Most students who open it finish both rounds, with a median time under six minutes.
  • It works on a phone, with a keyboard, and inside a Canvas page.

How it gets into Canvas

The activity is a single file. It can be uploaded to Course Files and embedded on any page with an iframe, or hosted on the library site and embedded by link, so a fix reaches every course at once. It does not write to the gradebook. If evidence of participation is needed, pair it with a zero-point quiz question as the course template already does.
